Pricing

Clear pricing based on task type, size, complexity, and deadline.

Basic

Presentations / Assignments / Reports

Best for short academic tasks, small presentations, and simple written work.

  • Task Size: Short (1–2 pages / 5–10 slides)
  • Delivery: 4–6 hours
  • Price Range: 8,000 – 30,000 HUF

Standard

Coursework & Assignments

Best for basic essays, exercises, and larger class submissions.

  • Task Size: Simple coursework / basic essays / exercises
  • Delivery: 6–12 hours
  • Price Range: 18,000 – 50,000 HUF

Most Popular

Thesis Preparation / Literature Review

Best for proposal writing, small thesis sections, structured research support, and academic guidance.

  • Task Size: Proposal / small section (5–10 pages)
  • Delivery: 1–2 weeks
  • Price Range: 10,000 – 60,000 HUF

Editing

Proofreading / Evaluation

Best for polishing grammar, structure, presentation, and readability.

  • Task Size: Short text (1,000–2,000+ words)
  • Delivery: 24–48 hours
  • Price Range: 10,000 – 25,000 HUF

Premium

Full Thesis Work

Best for long-term academic support with guidance, structure, section drafting, and full project coordination.

  • Support Period: Up to 1 month
  • Includes: Guidance, structure, section drafting, framework, comprehensive support
  • Pricing: Custom quote based on scope

Priority

Priority Services (All Categories)

Need faster delivery? Priority service is available across all categories.

  • Speed Upgrade: Express / urgent turnaround
  • Pricing Adjustment: 30–50% above standard rate
  • Best For: Tight deadlines and urgent submissions

Final pricing depends on complexity, deadline, and project scope. Clear agreement is made before work begins.
